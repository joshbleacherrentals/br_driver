Bleacher Rentals · Driver App

Design System & Brand Book

Apple-style minimalism built around brand blue (#405DAA) and green (#328C61) accents. Every token, list, modal and control derives from the Slate palette, in both light and dark.

Palette · Slate

Foundations · shared across every palette

Grouped inset lists

Rows grouped into rounded cards floating on the grouped background, separated by hairline dividers that inset from the leading edge — never full-bleed lines.

Soft elevation, not borders

Surfaces lift with a faint two-layer shadow tinted toward the brand navy, not hard 1px outlines. In dark mode, elevation reads as a lighter surface.

Corner radius scale

Controls 12 · cards 16 · sheets 22. Consistent, never mixed at random.

SF typography & restrained accent

System font (SF Pro). Weight and size carry hierarchy. Primary accent (accent · #405DAA) — active states, links, main actions. Secondary accent (secondaryAccent · #328C61) — positive highlights, badges, success-adjacent UI. Use both sparingly — never as filler.

Typography · типографіка

Primary typeface — SF Pro (system)

The app uses the native system font via -apple-system / San Francisco on iOS and Roboto on Android. No custom font files — this guarantees perfect legibility, Dynamic Type support, and zero bundle cost. Hierarchy comes from weight and size, not from switching families.

Setting rules

  • Three weights only: Regular 400 · Semibold 600 · Bold 700. Avoid Medium/Heavy to keep the scale clean.
  • Tracking: tight -0.01em on titles ≥20pt; +0.08em uppercase on eyebrows & group labels.
  • Numbers: tabular-nums for times, dates, counts & anything in columns so digits align.
  • Line-height: ~1.3 for body, ~1.1 for large titles. Body measure ≤ ~40 chars on mobile.
  • Color: textPrimary for content, textSecondary for meta — never pure grey guesses.
